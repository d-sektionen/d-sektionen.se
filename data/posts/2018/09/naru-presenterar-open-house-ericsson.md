**Welcome to this year’s edition of OPEN HOUSE @ ERICSSON SITE LINKÖPING September 26th!**

Visit Ericsson Linköping for an inspiring evening! Let us show you bits and bytes of our ongoing Research and Development projects, as well as a glimpse of our Global ICT Center. And why not visit Ericsson Garage while you are here?

You will also have the chance to meet recruiting managers and HR experts since Ericsson are hiring both regular employees and students for master thesis.

Food and drinks will of course be served at the Open House!

Join us for a fun evening! Sign up today, or latest September 23rd.
