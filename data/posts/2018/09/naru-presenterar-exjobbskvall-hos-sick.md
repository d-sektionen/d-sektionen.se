### WRITE YOUR MASTER THESIS AT SICK!

We offer 10 Master thesis works in Linköping, and also 10 more at our headquarter in Germany, in cool areas such as: Computer Vision, Algorithms, Deep Learning, Computer Graphics, Robot Vision, FPGA-design, Usability, Augmented Reality and Product Management! Take the chance to gain international experience while focusing on cool technologies!

Come and join us for an evening event at SICK in Linköping where you will hear more about the Master thesis topics, hear about your opportunites abroad, learn more about our software development, get a demo of our products and also get something to eat and drink! <!--more--> 25th of September at 18.00 -21.00. Wallenbergs gata 4, Linköping.

This event is only for students in the 5th year.
