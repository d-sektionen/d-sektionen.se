We would like to invite the student union and all sektioner to the annual international student festival IDAY at Linköping University in Linköping, Sweden. This year we celebrate the 10th anniversary of the festival which has been one of the largest international student festivals in the region. The event is a gathering of all different nationalities at the university to display their own culture and traditions. At IDAY 2014, we had 22 different nationalities being represented at the event.

Date: 10 April, 2015 Time: 10:00 – 17:00 Venue: Campus Valla, Linköping University, Linköping, Sweden – 58183

More info: [IDAY Invitation](http://d-sektionen.se/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/IDAY-Invitation.pdf).
