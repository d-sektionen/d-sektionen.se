Keen to explore where mentorship could take you in your early tech career?

BCG Platinion, the tech strategy part of Boston Consulting Group (BCG), invites students and young professionals to join their Women in Digital Mentorship Program in Stockholm – a unique opportunity to grow, learn, and connect.

Throughout the program, you’ll:  
![:female-technologist:](images/1f469-200d-1f4bb@2x.png) Receive one-on-one mentoring from a BCG Platinion consultant  
![:brain:](images/1f9e0@2x.png) Strengthen your problem-solving and communication skills  
![:handshake:](images/1f91d@2x.png) Connect with a network of aspiring women in tech in group events  
![:speech\_balloon:](images/1f4ac@2x.png) Gain insights into a career in IT strategy consulting

BCG Platinion is now looking for new mentees in Sweden for the Spring 2026 program.

Ready to take the next step in your tech career with a mentor by your side? Learn more and apply by February 8: [https://on.bcg.com/women-in-digital](https://on.bcg.com/women-in-digital)

(BCG Platinion covers all travel and accommodation expenses from other parts of Sweden to Stockholm.)
