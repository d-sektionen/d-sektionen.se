Consumer behavior and customer expectations are rapidly changing, and so are we. The competitive landscape is being redrawn and digitalization is driving the need to transform and rethink. This presents us with many challenges, but more importantly, with many exciting opportunities!  

**  
Trainee programs within H&M Group** 

We are currently running two Trainee Programs within the H&M Group. If joining one of them, you will get a unique possibility to kick-start your career with us. 

**Business Tech Trainee**

The Business Tech organization combines AI, IT and Business Development and is an important part of the group. We deliver technological solutions to all our brands within H&M Group, aiming to exceed the expectations of our customers – all over the world.

**Digital Growth Trainee**

Today we´re reaching customers all around the world. Physical and digital channels are increasingly integrated, and the customer offer is being further optimized to ensure a relevant presence in each of our markets. As a trainee within our Digital Growth Program you'll be at the heart of the exciting shift towards an even more digital customer offer and experience. 

**Read more about both programs** [**HERE**](https://career.hm.com/content/hmcareer/en_se/student/education/hm-trainee-programs.html) **and come join us at our live session on December 8th 1.00-2.00PM CET [HERE](https://tinyurl.com/yyfhgzz3)**.

**Qualifications**   

We are looking for you who are curious and enjoy doing things differently. We want you to be brave, make your voice heard and question how things are done. We want you to rethink and try the unfamiliar with an agile mindset and the ability to welcome the unexpected. But most importantly of all, we want you to be you, take ownership of your work and give it your best.
