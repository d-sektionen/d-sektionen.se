Accedo söker personer till sitt traineeprogram och har beskrivit det med sina egna ord så här:

Trainee Program for Technology Whizzes and Media Gurus! Accedo are market leaders within the domain of the video experience and we are changing when, where and how millions of people are entertained across the globe. We are pioneers, working with bleeding edge technology and the coolest customers (Netflix, HBO, Spotify) to make this happen. Understanding our role in this industry transformation and the impact on viewers makes us proud. Our trainee program is an important component in our ability to develop the competence that we need to challenge our industry and contribute to our learning and sharing culture. We offer a fast track introduction to our business and solution areas. The program runs over a twelve-month period and is built around 4 rotation periods which offers a unique opportunity to build your internal network and interact with colleagues across the globe.
