Så kom och häng med på detta spännande event, vi ser fram emot att träffa er och visa er det nya studierummet!

Hey students! We are thrilled to announce that the new study room sponsored by Configura is opening at the university and you're invited to the informal opening event on Monday, the 3rd of April from 11:00 to 13:00.

This is the perfect opportunity to meet the Configura team and fellow students who are looking for a quiet space to study and get to know each other. The room is designed to provide a comfortable and focused environment for studying and group meetings.

In addition to getting to know each other and exploring the new space, we will also have a small competition with an exciting prize! So, make sure to be there and participate so you could walk away with a great reward.

So come and join us for this exciting event, we're looking forward to meeting you and showing you the new study room!
