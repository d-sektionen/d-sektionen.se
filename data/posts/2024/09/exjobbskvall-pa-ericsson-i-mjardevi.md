Master Thesis evening at Ericsson in Mjärdevi  
  
When: Wednesday September 25th at 17:30 - 20:30  
Where: Ericsson office at Datalinjen 4 in Mjärdevi  
  
Looking for a master’s thesis project? We are looking for students for our thesis projects within AI, Security, Hardware, Software Development, Software Architecture, Signal Processing and more.  
  
During the evening you will meet managers and supervisors looking for students to around 20 thesis projects. You will have the opportunity to discuss the proposals, and your own ideas, with experienced supervisors. Besides the opportunity to find your thesis project, you will of course get something to eat.  
  
Last day to sign up is September 22nd.
