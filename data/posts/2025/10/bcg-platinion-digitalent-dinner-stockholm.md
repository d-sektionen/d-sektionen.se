Curious about management consulting and passionate about tech?

BCG Platinion, the tech strategy part of Boston Consulting Group (BCG), invites you to their annual DigiTalent Dinner in Stockholm  
 on Thursday, November 6!

By combining business strategy with cutting-edge technology, BCG Platinion’s IT Consultants and IT Architects advise global clients  
 on their tech agenda, guiding them toward a successful digital future.

The event kicks off at the BCG Stockholm office with a presentation, including insights into daily IT consulting life, international projects, and diverse career journeys. This is followed by a three-course dinner and drinks at a nearby restaurant, where you will have the opportunity to ask questions and connect with both BCG Platinion consultants and fellow participants.

Learn more and apply by Sunday, October 26 to secure your spot:  
[https://on.bcg.com/dinner](https://on.bcg.com/dinner)

Travel expenses from other parts of Sweden to Stockholm will be covered by BCG Platinion.
