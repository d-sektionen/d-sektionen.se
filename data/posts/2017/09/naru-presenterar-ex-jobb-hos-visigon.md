Shaping Tomorrow's Financial Markets

Are you starting your master thesis or pursuing a career within finance? Join us at Visigon!

A career at Visigon means you will be part of a young enterprise in an industry undergoing significant change. We offer career paths as expert roles in technology, project management and capital markets. Visigon is a financial IT consultancy firm established in Stockholm, and are actively looking for new colleagues with a strong technical background and with an interest for finance to help us transform the financial sector. We also offer a chance to do your master thesis at Visigon, either based on your ideas or through suggestions by us.

Job Applications are sent to career@visigon.se

For more information regarding master thesis and the company please visit our website www.visigon.com/en/career
