### Vewd Software, [https://www.vewd.com/](https://www.vewd.com/), is looking for exceptional developers to join us for 2-3 months during the summer of 2019.

Are you passionate about software development? Are you comfortable in at least one major programming language? Can you come up with creative solutions to unexpected problems? If so, we’d like to hear from you, we have some interesting things for you to help us with.

You’ll spend the summer with some of the best engineers in the industry and contribute to products that reach millions of users around the world. Vewd provides an international environment, modern development methods and nice central office in Linköping.<!--more-->

For immediate consideration, apply online today using [https://vewd.bamboohr.co.uk/jobs/view.php?id=32](https://vewd.bamboohr.co.uk/jobs/view.php?id=32) and tell us why you should work for Vewd Software. If possible provide links to source code for any of your projects. The application deadline is May 1st, 2019.

We also accepting candidates for BcS/McS thesis projects. Please apply online via [https://vewd.bamboohr.co.uk/jobs/view.php?id=35](https://vewd.bamboohr.co.uk/jobs/view.php?id=35) with a CV and ideas to discuss.

Vewd is hiring for permanent positions in Linköping. See details in: [https://www.vewd.com/job/c-developer-3/](https://www.vewd.com/job/c-developer-3/) [https://www.vewd.com/job/senior-javascript-developer/](https://www.vewd.com/job/senior-javascript-developer/)
