### TASK: MONITORING

For our IT operation we rely on effective monitoring to be able to act quickly and proactively on problems. We base our monitoring on Icinga which in turn is based on Nagios. Today we mostly collect and monitor basic metrics like network connectivity, cpu and disc utilization. However, in many scenarios those kinds of checks can indicate a healthy system while in fact the service is down. We would like to improve our monitoring by developing a framework for functional checks. Checks that really tests that the service is up and running. These checks should be fairly simple to write for an IT Operations specialist with very limited experience of programming.<!--more-->

The summer project members will work as a scrum team and choose the best frameworks and languages to solve the problem. The goal of the project is to develop the framework and checks for our critical internal systems as well as for the customer systems that we offer as a service. During the project you will gain knowledge in Icinga, Nagios, HTML, CSS, Ruby and Java.

The project will be conducted by three students over a period of eight weeks during the summer. Please submit your application before the end of February by visiting our website; [https://www.idainfront.se/karriar/lediga-tjanster/](https://www.idainfront.se/karriar/lediga-tjanster/)
