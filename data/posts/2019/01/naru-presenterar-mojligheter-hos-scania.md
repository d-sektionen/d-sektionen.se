### Join us on our journey towards a Sustainable Transport System

If you as a student within Datateknologsektionen at Linköping University, want to be a part of our journey towards a Sustainable Transport System, here are some more information about how to join us.<!--more-->

Scania is a world-leading provider of transport solutions. Together with our partners and customers we are driving the shift towards a sustainable transport system. Today, Scania has more than 300,000 connected vehicles. This number is rapidly increasing as the communicator is now standard in vehicles delivered to 55 of our markets. As digitalisation advances, Scania is positioning its trucks, buses and engine installations in ‘ecosystems’ of integrated mobility.

For summer jobs and thesis work please visit:

[https://www.scania.com/group/en/section/career/students/](https://www.scania.com/group/en/section/career/students/)

For those of you studying your 4th year (first year of master) the Scania Student Intro program is open for application at:

[https://www.scania.com/group/en/section/career/graduates/](https://www.scania.com/group/en/section/career/graduates/)

For those of you studying your 5th year (second year of master) or 3rd year Bachelor, the Scania Engineer Program is open for application at:

[https://www.scania.com/group/en/scania-engineer-program/](https://www.scania.com/group/en/scania-engineer-program/)

For those of you studying your 5th year (second year of master) the Scania Global Champion Trainee Program is open for application at:

[https://www.scania.com/group/en/scania-global-champion-trainee-program/](https://www.scania.com/group/en/scania-global-champion-trainee-program/)

If you are about to take your final exams and are ready for work please go to our career site and check out all our available positions:

[https://www.scania.com/group/en/available-positions/](https://www.scania.com/group/en/available-positions/)

For questions regarding ways career opportunities or ways to join Scania, please contact us at: [student@scania.com](mailto:student@scania.com)
