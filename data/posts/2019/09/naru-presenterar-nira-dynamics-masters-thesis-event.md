If you have a hard time finding ideas for your next Master's thesis, stay tuned. On October 8th, NIRA Dynamics will invite you to an event where you can meet the people behind the products at NIRA and talk about future possibilities and apply for your next Master's thesis.

Or, maybe you have your own ideas on a thesis, anywhere from cloud to onboard algorithms, then this is a great chance to talk about those ideas. Maybe we can make them happen together.

Among the customers are leading vehicle manufacturers as Audi, Volkswagen, Honda, Seat, Skoda, Volvo, Fiat and Renault.
