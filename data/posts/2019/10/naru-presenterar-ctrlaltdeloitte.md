#### Wondering about how Deloitte work within technology? Apply for Ctrl+Alt+Deloitte in Linköping!

During the event Ctrl+Alt+Deloitte you will get an insight on what a career with Deloitte looks like and the opportunities created when working with strategy, transformation and performance across various industries. You will also learn more about various tech topics and how we work with them. We finish off the day with a light dinner together. The event will be held in several cities and next up is Linköping.

When, where and how?  
Date: November 19th  
Time: 17-21  
Location: Deloitte’s Linköping Office, Storgatan 24

Apply here: [https://deloitte.recman.se/job.php?job\_id=108102](https://deloitte.recman.se/job.php?job_id=108102)
